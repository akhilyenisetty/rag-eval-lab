# IronLift Robotics, Product Specification Sheet (distractor document)

IronLift Robotics is a competitor to Acme Robotics. These specifications describe IronLift products only.

## IronLift L200

The IronLift L200 is an autonomous mobile robot for warehouse aisles. It carries loads up to 200 kg. Battery life is 10 hours on a full charge, and a full recharge takes 2 hours. Swappable battery packs are available.

## IronLift L500

The IronLift L500 is a heavy-duty pallet mover that carries loads up to 500 kg. Battery life is 6 hours, and it supports opportunity charging at docking stations between tasks.

## IronLift support plans

Basic support covers 9am to 5pm Eastern Time on weekdays. Priority support provides around-the-clock coverage with a 4-hour response target for severity-one incidents.

## Company

IronLift Robotics was founded in 2018 in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and has about 300 employees across two offices.
