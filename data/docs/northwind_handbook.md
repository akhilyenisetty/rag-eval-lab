# Northwind Logistics, Employee Handbook (distractor document)

This handbook applies only to employees of Northwind Logistics. It is a separate company from Acme Robotics.

## About Northwind

Northwind Logistics was founded in 2009 and is headquartered in Columbus, Ohio. The company runs regional freight and fulfillment centers. It employs roughly 1,200 people across six offices and eleven warehouses.

## Paid time off

Full-time Northwind employees receive 15 days of paid time off per year, plus 8 paid holidays. PTO starts accruing after a 90-day probation period. Up to 10 unused PTO days can be carried into the next calendar year.

## Part-time employees

Part-time Northwind employees working at least 20 hours per week receive prorated PTO, calculated as 7.5 days per year. Part-time staff are not eligible for carryover.

## Parental leave

Northwind offers 12 weeks of paid parental leave to birth and adoptive parents after six months of employment. Leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first year.

## Office hours

The Columbus headquarters is open 7:30am to 5:30pm Eastern Time on weekdays. Warehouses operate in three shifts, seven days a week.

## Benefits

Northwind offers medical, dental, and vision coverage, a 401(k) match of 4 percent, and optional pet insurance through a third-party provider.
