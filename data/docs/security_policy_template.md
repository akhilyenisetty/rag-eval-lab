# Generic IT Security Policy Template (distractor document)

This is a fill-in-the-blank template for small businesses. It is not the policy of any specific company.

## Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access and for administrator accounts. MFA is optional for standard user accounts on the internal office network, though it is recommended. Passwords must be at least 14 characters.

## Data handling

Data is classified as public, internal, or confidential. Confidential data should be encrypted in transit; encryption at rest is recommended where the storage system supports it.

## Access control

Access is granted by department managers on request. Access rights are reviewed once per year, and accounts of departed employees are disabled within 5 business days.

## Devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 10 minutes. Personal devices may access email only through an approved mobile management app.
