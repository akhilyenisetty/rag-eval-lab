# Acme Robotics, Employee & Product Handbook (sample knowledge base)

This is sample content so the RAG app has something factual to answer questions about. The
evaluation set (Stage 3) is written against the specific facts below.

## Company
Acme Robotics was founded in 2014 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas. The company builds
warehouse automation robots. As of 2025 it has about 680 employees across four offices.

## Products
- **Acme Mover 100**: an autonomous floor robot that transports loads up to 100 kg. Battery life
  is 8 hours on a full charge, and it recharges fully in 90 minutes.
- **Acme Mover 300**: a heavier unit that carries up to 300 kg, with a 6-hour battery life.
- **Acme Fleet Manager**: the software platform that coordinates robots, schedules charging, and
  reports uptime. It is billed per robot, per month.

## Support
Standard support covers business hours, 8am to 6pm Central Time, Monday through Friday. Premium
support adds 24/7 coverage and a 1-hour response-time guarantee for critical issues.

## Warranty
All robots include a 2-year hardware warranty. The warranty does not cover damage from water or
from operating the robot outside its rated load.

## Time off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per year, plus 10 company holidays. PTO
begins accruing on the first day of employment and unused PTO can roll over up to a maximum of 5 days.

## Security
Employees must enable multi-factor authentication on all company accounts. Customer data is
encrypted at rest and in transit, and access follows least-privilege by default.
