# Buying Guide: Choosing a Warehouse Robot (distractor document)

A general industry guide. It does not describe any one manufacturer's products.

## Payload classes

Warehouse robots are usually grouped by payload. Light units move totes and small cartons under 150 kg. Medium units handle 150 to 400 kg, which covers most single pallets. Heavy units move 400 kg and above, often with forklift-style attachments.

## Battery and charging

Typical battery life ranges from 6 to 12 hours. Fast-charging models recharge in about an hour, while standard models take two to four hours. Opportunity charging, where robots top up during idle moments, can keep a fleet running across multiple shifts.

## Support and maintenance

Most vendors offer a business-hours support tier and a premium tier with round-the-clock coverage. When comparing vendors, ask for the guaranteed response time on critical outages, not just the support hours.

## Questions to ask vendors

How heavy is your heaviest supported load? How long does a full recharge take? What does support cost after the first year? How many customer sites are running your robots today?
